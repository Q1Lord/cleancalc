Why most cleaning quotes are guesses

New cleaning businesses usually price one of three ways: copying a competitor, charging an hourly rate that ignores drive time, or undercutting until the work stops paying. All three fail for the same reason — nobody wrote down how long the job actually takes.

This site does two things. The calculator turns square footage, rooms and frequency into a defensible price range and an hours estimate you can check against your own experience. The guides cover the parts that decide whether the business survives: pricing, first customers, deposits, and what to say when a customer disputes a charge.

What this site is not

It is not a franchise pitch, a course, or a software review farm. There is no email gate on the calculator, no account, and nothing to install. If a guide recommends a tool, it says why, what it costs, and when you do not need it.

Estimates, not quotes. Cleaning prices vary enormously by city, building type and access. Use the calculator to get a starting point, then call three local cleaners and ask what they charge for the same job — that call is worth more than any calculator.
