About CleanCalc

A small site with one job: help new residential cleaning businesses price work properly.

Who writes this

CleanCalc is an independent project. The guides are written with AI assistance and edited before publication; the calculator is plain JavaScript that runs in your browser. We do not claim to be a cleaning company, a franchise, or a software vendor.

How the content is made

  • Pricing advice is built from publicly reported market ranges and the arithmetic shown on the page, so you can check it yourself.
  • Every number that could vary by city is presented as a range, not a fact.
  • Nothing here is legal, tax or insurance advice. For those, talk to a professional in your state.

Corrections

If a number is wrong or a recommendation is bad, say so and it gets fixed. Corrections are noted at the bottom of the page they affect.

Contact

This site is maintained by one person. Corrections, disagreements and "you got this number wrong" messages are all welcome — a contact address is being set up and will appear here once the site has a permanent domain. Until then, nothing on this site collects your details anyway.
