Privacy policy

Short version: the calculator runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing by default. There are no accounts, no cookies for tracking, and no form submissions on this site. The pricing calculator computes locally in JavaScript; the numbers you enter never leave your device.

Our hosting provider may keep standard access logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and abuse prevention. Those logs are not used to build profiles.

Analytics

If privacy-friendly analytics are added later, they will be cookieless and aggregate-only, and this page will say so before they go live.

Contact

No personal data is collected, so there is nothing to request, export or delete. If a contact address is added later, this page will be updated first.
