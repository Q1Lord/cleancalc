How the estimate is built

The calculator does not hide a magic number. It estimates labour hours from the size and room count, multiplies by an hourly rate you control, and adds a premium for one-off and heavy jobs. Changing the hourly rate changes everything — which is the point: your rate is a business decision, not a secret.

Before you quote a real customer

  • Time yourself once. Clean your own home with a stopwatch. Your estimate will be wrong by 20–40% until you have done this.
  • Add drive time and supplies. Neither is in the calculator. A two-hour clean 25 minutes away is a three-hour block of your day.
  • Quote a range, not a number. "Between $140 and $170, confirmed after I see the place" protects you when the house is worse than described.
  • Never quote from a photo alone. Photos hide clutter, pets, and how long it has been since the last clean.
Not sure about your market? Ask three local cleaners what they charge for the same size home, then position yourself deliberately — not accidentally.
